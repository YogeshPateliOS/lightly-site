Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Tealight is a private journal. This policy explains, in plain words, what happens to your information.

What we collect

Nothing. Tealight has no account, no server for your journal, and no analytics or advertising tools. We, the makers of Tealight, cannot see, read or access anything you write.

Where your journal lives

Everything you write (entries, feelings, reflections, conversations, released worries, lists, and your optional answer to "What brings you here?") is kept in Tealight on your device, protected by your device's encryption and, if you turn it on in Settings, by Face ID or your passcode.

If your device makes backups (for example iCloud Backup, or a backup to your computer), those backups can include Tealight's data, the same as other apps. They belong to you and are handled by Apple or your computer under your own account and settings. We have no access to them.

Reflections

Reflections are written privately by Apple's AI (Apple Intelligence), on supported devices. When you are online, Tealight may use Apple's Private Cloud Compute model for a deeper reply: your words are used only to write that reply, are not stored, and cannot be seen by Apple or by us. Otherwise the reply is written by Apple's on-device model, on your device. You can turn Apple's private cloud off in Settings → Privacy. On other devices, Tealight shows short replies written in advance by people. Nothing you write is ever sent to us, and nothing is used to train any model.

Talk it through (conversations)

If you talk something through in Tealight, Tealight's replies are written privately by Apple's AI in the same way: by Apple's Private Cloud Compute model when available (not stored, not seen by Apple or by us), otherwise on your device. Your messages and the replies are kept only on your device, with your journal. Nothing is sent to us, and nothing is used to train any model. Every message is checked on your device for words of crisis first; if they appear, Tealight shows helplines instead of replying. You can delete a conversation at any time, and Delete everything removes them all.

Apple Health (optional)

Only if you allow it, Tealight saves your evening mood and the feeling words you chose (never your writing) to Apple Health on your device. Tealight does not read any other Health data. Health data is managed by Apple under your Health settings and is never used for advertising.

Reminders (optional)

If you turn on the evening reminder, your device shows a notification. It never contains anything you wrote.

Backups you make

If you create a backup in Tealight, it is encrypted on your device with a passphrase you choose and saved wherever you decide (for example Files, iCloud Drive or AirDrop). We cannot open your backups, and we cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.

Exports and sharing

If you export your journal as text or PDF, it goes only where you send it.

Important updates

Once a day at most, Tealight may read a small public configuration file hosted with Apple CloudKit, to learn whether an important update is available. This sends no personal information and nothing you wrote.

Purchases

Tealight Plus subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details. You can manage or cancel in Settings → Tealight Plus → Manage subscription, or in your Apple Account settings.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They never include your journal.

Children

Tealight is not directed to children under 13.

Your choices

You can export or delete everything at any time in Settings → Delete everything. Deleting the app also removes its data from the device.

Not medical care

Tealight is a wellbeing journal, not a medical or therapy service. If you are in crisis, please contact your local emergency number or a crisis line. Tealight shows helplines for your region when it notices words of crisis, and findahelpline.com lists services in many countries.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and describe the change in the app's release notes.
