Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Lightly is a private journal. This policy explains, in plain words, what happens to your information.

What we collect

Nothing. Lightly has no account, no server for your journal, and no analytics or advertising tools. We, the makers of Lightly, cannot see, read or access anything you write.

Where your journal lives

Everything you write (entries, feelings, reflections, released worries and lists) is kept in Lightly on your device, protected by your device's encryption and, if you turn it on in Settings, by Face ID or your passcode.

If your device makes backups (for example iCloud Backup, or a backup to your computer), those backups can include Lightly's data, the same as other apps. They belong to you and are handled by Apple or your computer under your own account and settings. We have no access to them.

Reflections

Reflections are written by Apple's on-device language model (Apple Intelligence), on supported devices. Your writing is processed on your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. On other devices, Lightly shows short replies written in advance by people.

Apple Health (optional)

Only if you allow it, Lightly saves your evening mood and the feeling words you chose (never your writing) to Apple Health on your device. Lightly does not read any other Health data. Health data is managed by Apple under your Health settings and is never used for advertising.

Reminders (optional)

If you turn on the evening reminder, your device shows a notification. It never contains anything you wrote.

Backups you make

If you create a backup in Lightly, it is encrypted on your device with a passphrase you choose and saved wherever you decide (for example Files, iCloud Drive or AirDrop). We cannot open your backups, and we cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.

Exports and sharing

If you export your journal as text or PDF, it goes only where you send it.

Important updates

Once a day at most, Lightly may read a small public configuration file hosted with Apple CloudKit, to learn whether an important update is available. This sends no personal information and nothing you wrote.

Purchases

Lightly Plus subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details. You can manage or cancel in Settings → Lightly Plus → Manage subscription, or in your Apple Account settings.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They never include your journal.

Children

Lightly is not directed to children under 13.

Your choices

You can export or delete everything at any time in Settings → Delete everything. Deleting the app also removes its data from the device.

Not medical care

Lightly is a wellbeing journal, not a medical or therapy service. If you are in crisis, please contact your local emergency number or a crisis line. Lightly shows helplines for your region when it notices words of crisis, and findahelpline.com lists services in many countries.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and describe the change in the app's release notes.
