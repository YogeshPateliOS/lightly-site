Help

Questions people ask. If yours isn't here, write to yogeshp614@gmail.com and include the version from Settings → About.

If you're struggling right now
Please call your local emergency number, or find a free helpline at findahelpline.com. Lightly is a journal, not a crisis service.

Can anyone read my journal?

We can't: there's no account and nothing you write is sent to us. It's kept on your device. Anyone who can open your unlocked phone could open the app, so you can turn on Settings → Lock with Face ID.

What if I lose or change my phone?

Make a backup in Settings → Your journal → Back up. On the new device, install Lightly and choose Restore from backup. Your device's own backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) can also bring Lightly back with the rest of your apps.

I forgot my backup passphrase

We can't recover it; that's what keeps your backup private. Make a new backup with a new passphrase.

Why don't I see AI reflections?

AI reflections need a device with Apple Intelligence turned on. On other devices, Lightly shows short, kind replies written in advance. After your first week, AI reflections are part of Lightly Plus; without it you still get a written reply every night.

What's free?

Writing, naming feelings, releasing worries, breathing, setting tomorrow down, your lamps, Calendar, search, backup and export are always free. Nothing you've written is ever locked away.

What does Lightly Plus add?

AI reflections after every entry, the rest of your Journey (then and now, battles you've won) and a letter each month in your own words. Your first week of writing includes everything.

How do I cancel Lightly Plus?

Settings → Lightly Plus → Manage subscription, or your Apple Account settings. Cancelling never deletes anything you wrote.

Is Lightly therapy?

No. It's a journal for everyday wellbeing. If you're struggling, please reach out to a professional or a crisis line.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete everything. This can't be undone unless you made a backup.
